THE RULES ARE SIMPLE

Make every
blast count.

Beat 10 named CPU opponents in one run. A hit knocks an opponent out instantly. If your blast hits the floor, a wall, or flies away without hitting an opponent, your run ends. Getting hit by a CPU blast also ends your run instantly.

Rookie helps your aim when you are already facing a nearby opponent. Pro uses manual aim. Orange warnings appear before CPUs launch; move sideways, dodge, or hide behind the colored walls. Dodging moves you quickly but does not make you invincible.

W/S or up/down: move · A/D or left/right: turn · right-click and drag: aim · left-click or J: poop blast · Q: dodge · Space: jump · Shift: sprint · V: eye/chase view · Esc: pause. On phones, steer with the joystick, drag the scene to aim, and tap the action buttons.

Earn 1 Archerbox coin per knockout. At 10 knockouts in one run, you win Golden Underwear to wear over your clothes in Poop Panic. Unlocks and records save on this browser.
