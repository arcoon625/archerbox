YOUR MISSION BRIEFING

Outsmart the security.

01 / FIND THE CARDS

Walk into the floating cyan and amber keycards. You need both to unlock the glowing exit.

02 / READ THE BEAMS

Jump over low lasers. Hold C or Ctrl to duck under high ones. Moving scanners change height. Pulse gates go dark between bursts: wait for green emitters.

03 / USE YOUR TOOLS

Stand by a relay and press E to turn off lasers for seven seconds. Q fires your EMP pulse wherever you are. It recharges after use.

04 / KEEP YOUR COOL

A zap sends you to a checkpoint and adds three seconds. Your cards stay collected. There are unlimited retries. Every completed mission earns one Archerbox coin.

W/S or up/down: move · A/D or left/right: turn your view · drag mouse: look · Space: jump · C / Ctrl: crouch · Shift: run · E: relay · Q: EMP · M: map · R: checkpoint · Esc: pause. On phones, steer your view with the joystick and use the action buttons.
