    YOUR RANGER HANDBOOK

    Daylight is a gift.
    Use it.

    01

    Start at your base.Daylight lasts exactly 4 minutes. Your free Hunting Rifle is ready with H. Deer, rabbits and sheep roam the forest: aim and shoot, then press E near their food bundle. Press R beside a lit campfire to cook one raw food into a meal (5 seconds); V eats a meal for +45 hunger and +12 health. Start with one packed meal. Berries restore 12 hunger and regrow daily. Hunger only falls while playing. Press X for your free axe. Aim at a tree and click or press J three times. Press E beside its logs to collect 3 wood. Press E near the campfire to add one log (+2 minutes). Start with two logs.

    02

    The walls vanish at night.Night one is safe: the bear will NOT attack. From night two onward, it hunts for 2 minutes. Walk or sprint to a can and press E to climb inside. Keep the fire burning: if it runs out, ALL cans disappear and you are forced out of hiding! Refuel to bring them back. Trees regrow at dawn. A brief bear jumpscare plays if it catches you.

    03

    Camp goals and mountain trails.Every day, gather 6 wood (+5 diamonds), collect 3 food from hunts (+8), and cook 2 meals (+5). Goals reset at dawn. Every full night earns 5 diamonds and 1 Archerbox coin. Survive night 100 for another 100 diamonds. Spend diamonds on weapons at the daytime shop. Every day, mountain rewards unlock when the daylight timer reaches 2:00. Climb each marked summit for 10 diamonds, once per mountain per day before nightfall. A golden chest arrives at base every two survived days; press E beside it for a free gun roll. Prizes stay in your backpack, and duplicates give 10 diamonds.
