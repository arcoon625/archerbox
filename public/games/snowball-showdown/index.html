❄ MAKE IT COUNT

Three snowball hits knock a player out. Your team scores a point, and that player respawns after 3 seconds with a brief protective ring. Teammates cannot hit each other.

▤ WORK THE COVER

Forts and cabins stop snowballs. Move around them to get an open shot. Jump over low snow walls. Frozen Lake has slippery ice, so leave room to stop.

↗ DODGE & REPACK

Press Q to dash out of the way. Dodges recharge in 2.4 seconds. You carry six snowballs; R packs a fresh handful. An empty throw starts packing too.

★ WIN THE SNOW DAY

1v1 rounds go to 3 knockouts, 2v2 to 5, and 4v4 to 7. Lead after 90 seconds to win; a tie becomes sudden death. Win two rounds for the match. Each round won earns 1 Archerbox coin.

WASD: move · mouse: aim · click or hold J: throw · right-click: aim closer · arrows: move and turn · Space: jump · Shift: sprint · Q: dodge · R: pack · V: eye / chase view · Esc: pause. Click the arena to capture the mouse; drag to aim if your browser does not capture it.

On phones use the steering joystick and action buttons; drag the right side to look. Easy mode helps your aim when the crosshair is close to an opponent. All teammates and opponents are CPUs.
