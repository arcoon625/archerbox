YOUR FIELD GUIDE

Read the forecast.
Make your move.

≈ FLASH FLOOD

Climb the stone ridge or walk up the lookout stairs. Water rises during the round. If your head goes underwater, your air runs out before your health starts dropping.

↻ TORNADO

Watch the funnel and keep your distance. Strong winds can pull you in. The concrete shelter reduces the wind, but cabins and the lookout can collapse.

✦ METEOR SHOWER

Move away from the red warning circles, or get beneath a sturdy roof. Repeated strikes can destroy the shelter. A roof that has collapsed cannot protect you.

ϟ EARTHQUAKE

Stay on open ground. Buildings flash amber before they collapse. Get off rooftops and move away from walls and falling debris.

W/S or up/down: move · A/D or left/right: steer your view · Space: jump · Shift: run · drag: look · V: chase / eye view · Esc: pause. On phones, use the steering joystick and action buttons.

A surprise expedition has four different disasters. Choose a single disaster to practice one round. Survive a round to earn 1 Archerbox coin. Green first-aid kits restore health. CPU survivors try to find safety too.
