SMART MOVES WIN BATTLES

A little strategy.
A lot of adventure.

W A S D / ARROWSMove

Circle your rival. Face an opponent to land your strike. Mouse look or drag turns the camera.

J / LEFT CLICKStrike

Hold for repeated light attacks. Weapons have different reach, speed, and power.

E / RIGHT CLICKBlock & parry

Face an attack to block it. Raise your guard just before it lands for a parry that stuns the attacker.

QHeavy attack

Heavy attacks take longer and use more stamina, but hit harder and chip through a shield.

SHIFT / SPACEDodge

Dodge in the direction you’re moving. You briefly avoid hits. It costs stamina and needs time to recharge.

V / ESCCamera & pause

Choose chase or eye view. Esc pauses. Win a round to earn 1 Archerbox coin. First to two wins a match; Survival has five waves.
