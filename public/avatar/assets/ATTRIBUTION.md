# Avatar head scan credit

Infinite, 3D Head Scan by **Lee Perry-Smith**, based on work at www.triplegangers.com.

Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The avatar adapts the original geometry by cropping the bust, fitting it to the Archerbox rig, adding eye openings and eye geometry, deforming expressions, recoloring the skin and adding separate hair and wardrobe items. The bundled color and tangent normal maps come from the same source. The original license text is retained in LeePerrySmith_License.txt.
