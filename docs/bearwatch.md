# Bearwatch: 100 Nights

A solo, first-person Three.js survival game bundled at `public/games/bearwatch/`, registered in the Archerbox catalog and featured in the lobby. The cover is a screenshot of the game's own bear, forest, camp and trash can. No external assets or services are required by the game.

Daylight is exactly 240 seconds of active game time; night is exactly 120. The player spawns at Pine Hollow base. Daytime log walls are solid. At night their renderer and collisions both disappear, and the bear prowls or chases a visible player. The four-legged bear steers around obstacles, attacks with a cooldown, can be stunned, and retreats for 18 seconds after its courage/health meter is exhausted. There is no gore. Daylight never causes bear damage.

Eight green trash cans are hiding spots powered by the campfire. The fire starts with 300 seconds of fuel and two carried logs. X toggles a free woodcutting axe. Three aimed swings chop a tree into a bundle of three logs; E collects it. E near the fire consumes one log and adds 120 seconds, up to 480 seconds. Fuel only burns during active play. At zero, all cans disappear visually and lose collision and interaction; a hidden player is immediately exposed. Refueling restores them. Trees regrow at dawn except directly beneath the player or bear. Resources, partial chops and dropped logs save. E enters or exits a nearby can; hiding freezes movement and prevents attacks. Players may stay hidden through dawn while the fire remains lit. A short, non-graphic bear close-up plays after a fatal catch, respects sound and reduced-motion preferences, and can be skipped. The nighttime camera looks out through a shaded opening; the player's own can is hidden from that camera to avoid clipping into it. A flashlight, map, sprint stamina and jumping help navigation. Mouse drag or captured-mouse look, keyboard, and on-screen phone controls are available. WASD strafes; left/right arrows turn.

Each successful night credits exactly 5 local diamonds and queues 1 Archerbox coin. Completing the hundredth night adds 100 bonus diamonds once and ends the expedition. Diamond-shop purchases are permanent: Camp Stick (free), Ranger Bat (5), Slingshot (20), Ranger Crossbow (50), Sleep Dart (100), Flare Launcher (180). Each has a distinct model, range, stun duration and cooldown. Ranged attacks require aim. The shop only opens in daylight near base; menus, pause, hidden browser tabs, and shopping suspend the clock. Losing restarts the expedition count while retaining diamonds, equipment, best night, and prior wins.

The campaign and separate diamond balance save under `archerbox-bearwatch-v1` on each phase change, purchase, hide/exit, pause and every five active seconds. Resume restores the exact saved phase and elapsed time, without offline advancement. Pending coin tickets persist before their asynchronous award; the existing wallet's permanent unique-event keys prevent duplicate payment after a refresh. No existing storage keys change. The campaign is solo; it does not advertise friend multiplayer.

Validation: `node tests/bearwatch.test.mjs` covers a complete 100-night simulation, exact phase boundaries, both visual-state and collision-state wall behavior, all cans and exits, daytime safety, attacks and all weapons, purchases, invalid input, persistence, failure and victory freezes, the bonus, duplicate-proof coins, and bear/weapon geometry. `node tests/coins.test.mjs` and `node tests/bundle.test.mjs` cover shared-wallet and catalog integration. Browser fixtures in ignored `.sites-runtime/` test real nightfall, dawn payment, buying/equipping, the victory overlay and hiding in an isolated in-memory storage iframe; fixtures are never published. A 390 × 844 iframe exercises the actual phone media queries and controls.

Opening or resuming an expedition re-reads newer saves from another tab. Older paused tabs do not write on close, and a save detects a newer snapshot before writing, protecting diamonds and progress from a stale tab. Browser QA includes a newer-save/resume scenario.

Three mountains use one continuous terrain-height function for rendering, walking, bear elevation and aiming. Daily diamond rewards unlock after exactly 120 daylight seconds (2:00 remaining), close at night, and reset at dawn: 10 diamonds per summit per day. Golden chests arrive every two complete survived nights, with a persistent notification and base marker. Earned chests accumulate across deaths and expeditions. A roll costs no diamonds: Trail Pistol 35%, Ranger Carbine 30%, Sleep Dart 20%, Flare Launcher 10%, Golden Rifle 5%. Duplicate guns give 10 diamonds. The award is saved before its reveal animation; refreshing or closing resumes the same prize without paying twice. Legacy saves receive their earned two-day drops and starter fire resources.

Additional validation: `node tests/bearwatch-expeditions.test.mjs` covers all actual uphill approaches, matching terrain, 50 two-day chest drops through night 100, every loot boundary, reward persistence and duplicate protection. `node tests/bearwatch-campfire.test.mjs` covers halfway-day rewards, daily reset, chopping, collecting, fuel costs, an occupied can vanishing, bear attacks after exposure, restored shelter, invalid saved resources and migration. Long campaign fixtures explicitly maintain fuel to isolate phase and reward checks. Browser QA additionally exercises chopping, relighting, forced exit, summit unlocks, a real chest reveal and the jumpscare-to-result transition using isolated in-memory storage.

## Wildlife and food

Night one is a safe practice night: `bearDanger` enables both rendering and attacks only from night two, including restored saves. Nine animals (three each of deer, rabbit and sheep) graze, wander and flee near a visible player or nearby gunshot. Each has four articulated legs and a distinct model. A free Hunting Rifle (H / slot 0) is provided for new and migrated saves without removing existing weapons. Hits require range, horizontal and vertical aim, and a clear line through trees, rocks, walls and terrain. Hunting produces a food bundle without blood; E collects 3 raw food for deer, 1 for rabbits or 2 for sheep. Harvested animals return at dawn; uncollected bundles remain.

Hunger drains 0.075 per active second. R beside a lit campfire reserves one raw food for a five-second cooking job, which pauses if the fire goes out and survives saving. V eats one meal (+45 hunger, +12 health), unless already nearly full. Starters and legacy saves receive full hunger and one meal. At zero hunger, health drains two points per second; starvation uses its own result message, without a bear jumpscare. Six berry bushes restore 12 hunger each once per day. Daily goals automatically grant 5 diamonds for collecting six wood, 8 for three food from hunting, and 5 for two cooked meals. Progress and claims save and reset at dawn. Menus, time away and paused states consume neither hunger nor cooking time.

The graphics use locally generated bark, fur, wool, earth and stone maps, open needle fronds instead of solid tree cones, grass blades, directional afternoon sunlight, and smaller firearm recoil. No remote model or texture dependency is added. `tests/bearwatch-wildlife.test.mjs` verifies the safe first night, second-night attacks, every species, aim and shot occlusion, harvest deduplication, food/cooking save recovery, goals, hunger and legacy migration, plus four-leg anatomy. Campaign reward tests explicitly maintain hunger and fire to isolate the 100-night reward calculations.
