# Pokémon: Meadow Quest

A fan-made solo Pokémon adventure bundled under `public/games/pokemon-meadow`. Original SVG character illustrations, a Canvas 2D world, accessible HTML menus and battle controls, and no remote game assets. It uses an Adventure category and 2.5D label in the Archerbox catalog. It is not presented as an official Pokémon release. The simplified game-specific levels, evolution timing and battle mechanics are explained in the field guide.

Choose Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle. Explore Willow Town, Clover Meadow, Sunstone Ridge and Azure Cove. Roaming Pokémon start encounters when approached and interacted with. Catching consumes a Poké Ball, is more likely at low HP, and grants a permanent Pokémon rather than a temporary battle sprite. A six-member team has a separate reserve; swapping a reserve Pokémon onto a full team moves the previous teammate into reserve. The Pokédex tracks 17 seen and collected species. Initial starters do not award coins. The first catch or evolution of another species awards one permanent discovery coin. Wild Dragonite unlocks after the champion victory so every Pokédex species can be collected.

Turn-based battles include type effectiveness, three attacks (the strongest unlocks at level 7), Guard, potions, switching and safe escape from wild battles. Trainer Pokémon cannot be caught, and trainer battles cannot be fled. Invalid actions do not spend a turn. Fainting automatically chooses another healthy teammate, or returns the whole team to town for recovery with no Pokémon lost. The Pokémon Center provides full healing and at least 20 balls and six potions for free. Battles and catches grant XP; starter Pokémon evolve at level 8 in this adventure. Luca, Maya and Rowan award three distinct badges; all three unlock Iris’s champion battle. Badge coins are first-win only and rematches remain available.

`archerbox-pokemon-meadow-v1` stores the team, reserve, HP, XP, bag, Pokédex, badges, encounter cooldowns and an in-progress battle. Each turn is saved before its visual animation; refreshing resumes committed state rather than replaying rewards. Queued discovery and badge tickets use the existing shared wallet’s permanent unique keys. Current saves are re-read before mutations to protect newer tabs; storage failure keeps the in-memory adventure and displays a notice. Time, movement and encounters pause in menus or hidden tabs. There are no paid purchases, remote API requirements, or multiplayer claims.

Validation: `node tests/pokemon-meadow.test.mjs` covers every starter, catching and failed turns, reward replay protection, saved battle recovery, restricted trainer actions, all three badges and champion, the Dragonite unlock, evolution, faint recovery, reserve swaps, invalid input and reachable approaches to all encounters and trainers. Coin and bundle tests cover all 20 catalog games. Isolated browser fixtures in ignored `.sites-runtime/` exercise the real starter UI, center healing and supplies, catching and Pokédex updates, switching, evolution and faint recovery. Phone QA uses a 390 × 844 iframe.
